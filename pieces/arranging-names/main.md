In 2012 for a freshman final paper I wrote about the arrangements of names on Maya Lin’s Vietnam Veterans Memorial and Michael Arad’s National September 11 Memorial.

I recall, while writing it, feeling for the first time like I was _doing research_: finding and connecting obscure sources in dusty corners of the web (no time for physical archives, it was the night before the deadline). Until then I assumed everything true had already been written.

It’s a student paper and shows it: some vocab clunkers, and a title that makes me cringe how hard I stretched for a pun. Eventually I hope to revisit and rewrite it, but today (on the 25th anniversary of 9/11/01) I’m preserving [the original PDF as I turned it in](./ingodwetruss.pdf).
